Our story

Meet Tabitha & Evie: students who love creating and sharing ideas.

We built this website to show how coding, design, and storytelling can make a positive difference. Our project is bright, warm, and proud of where we come from.

About us

We are two young creators from Kenya who love coding, design, and making websites that feel welcoming. This project has helped us learn new skills while supporting others through creative storytelling.

Since we started building together, we have been sharing our passions and helping others see how technology can be inspiring and empowering.

Our mission

To create projects that are beautiful, joyful, and easy to use. We want our website to feel friendly for everyone who visits.

Our story

We began by learning together and building pages for school. Over time, we realized our ideas could reach more people and make a positive impact.

Our values

We believe in creativity, kindness, and using our skills to help girls who face difficulties. Our work is about learning, expressing ourselves, and growing together.

Built with creativity and a bright future in mind.
