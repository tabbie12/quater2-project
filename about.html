About us

How we started and what we believe in.

We are Tabitha and Evie, two students who use design, words, and code to make a positive, creative space online.

Our mission

To create projects that feel bright, welcoming, and full of heart. We want our web pages to inspire visitors and show the power of learning together.

Our values

Creativity, kindness, and teamwork guide everything we do. We strive to build pages that are clear, cheerful, and easy for anyone to use.

Our future

We are learning and growing every day. Our goal is to keep building new stories, improving our skills, and sharing what we create with others.

Our journey

Start

We began as classmates exploring web design and discovered how much fun it is to build pages together.

Today

Now we share our ideas in a friendly website, highlighting our work, values, and the people behind the project.

Next

We want to keep learning, add more projects, and help others see how technology can support their dreams.

Built with care, creativity, and a bright future in mind.
